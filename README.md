**Concept explanation:**
“Teach One, Lift Many”
•	Represent a student's week into a grid of hours. Most squares stay empty, but two are highlighted in yellow: the two hours a week Roshan Raah asks for. 
•	A notebook page carries the campaign line, with "Many." present diffrently to show how one lesson grows.
•	The framed pictures, a teacher with two children and a child writing, show the classroom moment where learning begins, while the house and sun beside the grid show that moment reaching a whole family. 
•	The poster speaks to university students who feel they have little time or little to give. It shows that two ordinary hours are small enough to fit a schedule, and big enough to change a child's future. 
•	A checklist explains what volunteers do, and a clear button and QR code make signing up one step.
